# CYBERWARFARE 2036

### A Foresight Analysis of Humans, AI Agents & Autonomous Cyber Conflict

> What happens when artificial intelligence stops being merely a tool in cyberwarfare and becomes an active participant?

---

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is rapidly evolving from a passive analytical tool into increasingly capable systems that can reason, plan, and execute complex tasks.

This research explores how that transition could reshape cyber conflict over the next decade.

Rather than attempting to predict a single future, this project examines multiple plausible trajectories for the evolution of AI-driven cyber operations, autonomous defense, human-AI collaboration, and AI-versus-AI conflict.

The objective is to distinguish between what is already observable, what is emerging, what can reasonably be projected, and what remains speculative.

---

## Research Question

**At what point does AI stop being a tool in cyberwarfare and become an active participant in cyber conflict?**

---

## Scope

This research focuses on the potential evolution of:

- AI-assisted offensive security
- Autonomous cyber operations
- AI-driven vulnerability discovery
- Autonomous cyber defense
- AI-enabled social engineering
- AI agents and cyber operations
- Critical infrastructure security
- AI-versus-AI cyber conflict
- Human oversight and control
- Attribution and accountability

---

## Methodology

The analysis separates observations from projections using four levels:

| Level | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Observed** | Technologies or behaviors already demonstrated |
| **Emerging** | Developments showing early evidence of adoption or capability |
| **Projected** | Plausible developments based on current trajectories |
| **Speculative** | Possible futures with significant uncertainty |

The purpose is not to claim certainty, but to explore plausible outcomes and identify potential risks before they become reality.
