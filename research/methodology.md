# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This research examines how artificial intelligence could transform cyber conflict between 2026 and 2036.

The objective is not to predict the future with certainty, but to identify plausible developments by examining current capabilities, emerging trends, technological trajectories, and potential consequences.

## Analytical Framework

The research follows four levels of certainty:

### 1. Observed

Capabilities, technologies, or behaviors that have already been demonstrated or documented.

### 2. Emerging

Developments that are beginning to appear but have not yet reached widespread maturity or adoption.

### 3. Projected

Plausible developments derived from existing technological and strategic trends.

### 4. Speculative

Possible outcomes that cannot currently be supported with strong evidence but may become relevant under certain conditions.

## Core Principle

The further a scenario extends beyond currently demonstrated capabilities, the greater the uncertainty.

Therefore, every major projection should be evaluated against:

- Current technological capability
- Rate of technological development
- Economic feasibility
- Adoption incentives
- Defensive countermeasures
- Human decision-making
- Regulatory constraints
- Geopolitical factors

## Research Horizon

**Starting Point:** 2026  
**Target Horizon:** 2036

The analysis will be updated as new evidence becomes available.
